About

Mohammed Mohsen is a talented and multi-faceted artist, specializing in intense and emotionally-driven videography and filmmaking. He started his journey during the 2020 pandemic with a canon 200D, fascinated with architecture, motorsports and unique storytelling, he went from doing photography to moody videos in instagram. With his unique eye for details, he has moved on to make videos from friends and family to big names like Puma, Red Bull, Wello, Fiba Basketball, Project RE:SESH, Saif & Maryam videos on Tiktok and IG.
